01Brand EssenceReading the mark
VCCI unites, represents and protects the Vietnamese business community. Its identity must carry the weight of a national institution and the warmth of a trusted partner.
Form
Monumental geometric capitals — heavy, square‑shouldered, built from circles and clean cuts. Engineered stability.
Symbol
The Đông Sơn bronze‑drum sun: fourteen rays, ringed by figural bands — Vietnam's oldest emblem at the heart of the name.
Colour
State blue for authority and permanence; drum gold for prosperity, heritage and national pride.
Character
Authoritative · Trusted · Enduring · Proud. Formal, yet a partner — never bureaucratic.

09Visual ToneHow the brand feels
Authoritative.Large serif statements, firm alignments, generous margins.
Rooted.Heritage through the drum — quoted, never decorated.
Measured.One accent per surface. Quiet confidence over volume.
Open.Warm paper, real people, documentary light — never stock gloss.
Clarity over cleverness; the long view over the loud moment.
10Spacing & Consistency8‑point system
1Margins = 2x. Page margins equal twice the drum radius of the logo as placed.
2Blue logo on light grounds only. On dark fields, use the Paper plate — never white letters.
3One gold gesture per composition: a rule, a word, or the drum ring.
440% negative space minimum. Radius 4 px print, 8 px UI.
