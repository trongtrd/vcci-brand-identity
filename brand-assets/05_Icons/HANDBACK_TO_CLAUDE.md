# Handback to Claude — VCCI Icons v1

## Status

The owner approved Direction A (Geometric Clarity) and signed off the complete 48 UI / 24 hero vector set on 2026-10-05 with “duyệt bộ này.” This is the approved Version 1.0 release, packaged as VCCI_Icons_v1.zip.

## Approved direction

A restrained, frontal geometric line system with spacious compositions and one small gold accent behind blue linework. The approved raster sheet is `Sheets/VCCI_Hero_Concepts.png`. Final geometry is constructed manually rather than tracing the raster; ambiguous concepts are clarified for the policy and enterprise priorities.

## Approved deviations

Hero scope increased from 20 to 24, with 12 priority themes first. Numbering and slugs follow the revised catalog below. Startup is no longer a standalone v1 icon; law reform, policy dialogue and sustainability are refined into more specific themes. UI remains 48. Gold-on-dark has no accent. First-use labels are required unless supplied by an adjacent title. Primary use is Word, PowerPoint, reports and events, with common UI uses supported.

## Known issues and use limitations

No unresolved owner-requested changes remain. The owner approved the complete set, including heroes 02, 03, 04, 09 and 10. Abstract policy concepts remain label-dependent by design. Hero 24 px is a stress-test size; 48 px is the minimum recommended display. The concept sheet is a raster reference and not a source of production geometry. All automatic checks passed, and every icon was visually checked at 24, 48 and 128 px on Paper and VCCI Blue. Contact sheets include Deep Navy for heroes. Geometry is unchanged from the approved review set; only release status and contact-sheet footers were updated.
